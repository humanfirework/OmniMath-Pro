Three foundations. One language for change.

The derivative measures how a quantity changes at a single instant. The integral accumulates infinitely many such changes into a finite total. The limit is the rigorous scaffolding on which both are built — every continuous argument in mathematics passes through it.

A tangent line is the best linear approximation to a curve at a point — the line that "kisses" the graph and shares its direction.

For a function f, the derivative f′(x) at a point is the slope of the tangent line there. Formally, it is the limit of the difference quotient as the step h shrinks to zero:

Step-through proof

The derivative of sin(x) is cos(x).

01

Begin with the limit definition.

02

Apply the angle-addition identity for sine.

03

Split into two fundamental limits — both evaluated at h = 0.

04

Use the classical evaluations: limh→0 sin(h)/h = 1 and limh→0 (1 − cos(h))/h = 0.

05

Therefore, the derivative of sine is cosine. ∎

§ 04 / PRACTICE

Worked
Examples.

Three applied problems, each with progressive hints. Reveal one step at a time — only when you're ready.

Related rates · differentiation

A ladder slides down a wall.

A 10 ft ladder leans against a vertical wall. The bottom is pulled away at 2 ft/s. When the bottom is 6 ft from the wall, how fast is the top sliding down?
HINT 01Let x be the bottom distance, y the height. Pythagoras: x² + y² = 100.
HINT 02Differentiate implicitly with respect to time t.
HINT 03At x = 6: y = √(100 − 36) = 8. Plug in.
SOLVE2x·dx/dt + 2y·dy/dt = 0 ⟹ dy/dt = −(x/y)·dx/dt = −(6/8)(2) = −1.5 ft/s.
The top slides down at 1.5 ft/s.
Optimization · integration

Minimize the surface area of a can.

Design a cylindrical can to hold 355 mL (≈ 355 cm³) of liquid. What radius minimizes the total surface area (top, bottom, side)?
HINT 01Volume V = πr²h = 355. Surface S = 2πr² + 2πrh.
HINT 02Eliminate h: h = 355 / (πr²). Substitute into S.
HINT 03Set dS/dr = 0 to find the critical point.
SOLVEdS/dr = 4πr − 710/r² = 0 ⟹ r³ = 710/(4π) ≈ 56.5 ⟹ r ≈ 3.84 cm, h ≈ 7.67 cm.
r ≈ 3.84 cm, h ≈ 7.67 cm (height = diameter)
Limits · L'Hôpital

The indeterminate 0/0.

Evaluate limx→0 (eˣ − 1 − x) / x². Direct substitution gives 0/0.
HINT 01Apply L'Hôpital's rule: differentiate numerator and denominator separately.
HINT 02Numerator' = eˣ − 1, denominator' = 2x. Still 0/0 at x = 0.
HINT 03Apply L'Hôpital a second time.
SOLVE(eˣ − 1 − x)″ = eˣ, (x²)″ = 2. Limit = e⁰/2 = 1/2.
The limit equals 1/2.
Area between curves

Enclosed region between parabolas.

Find the area enclosed between y = x² and y = 2x − x².
HINT 01Find intersection points: x² = 2x − x² ⟹ 2x² − 2x = 0.
HINT 02Intersections at x = 0 and x = 1. On (0,1), 2x − x² > x².
HINT 03Integrate the difference: ∫₀¹ (2x − 2x²) dx.
SOLVE[x² − (2/3)x³]₀¹ = 1 − 2/3 = 1/3.
The enclosed area is 1/3 square unit.
